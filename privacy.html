Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Applies to mobile apps published by AB DIGITECH, Alen Brunec, s.p.

Who we are

AB DIGITECH, Alen Brunec, s.p. (“we”, “us”), Dolnja Bistrica 57B, 9232 Črenšovci, Slovenia, develops and publishes mobile games such as Bubble Balls. Contact: support@abdigitech.net or info@abdigitech.net.

What this covers

This policy describes how our apps handle information. Individual games may also link to a game-specific support or privacy page with extra detail. Our apps do not require an account and we do not operate a user-account database.

Advertising and Google services

Some of our apps (including Bubble Balls version 1.4 and later) use Google AdMob to show advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process data such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device or advertising identifiers (where permitted)
  • Ad interactions and app usage related to ads
  • Diagnostic data, including crash and performance information

These services use data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain security, and improve their services. Personalized ads depend on applicable consent and device permissions. Even non-personalized or limited ads may involve data processing.

We do not receive your advertising identifier directly from the game for our own profiling. Google processes advertising data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Advertising data may be processed outside your country under the safeguards described in Google’s policies.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows a privacy or consent message before advertising is initialized. Where available, use in-app privacy choices to review or change advertising preferences. You can refuse or withdraw consent without losing access to gameplay; the ads available to you may change.

Local data on your device

Apps may store limited data locally on your device (for example high scores, sound settings, or consent status). This data stays on the device unless a third-party SDK transmits related information as described above.

Children

Our apps are general-audience games. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own purposes. Advertising partners may apply their own age-related policies and restrictions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, or to object to certain processing. For advertising choices, use the in-app controls where available and your device’s advertising or privacy settings. For requests about our own processing, contact support@abdigitech.net.

Changes

We may update this policy when our apps or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the apps after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
